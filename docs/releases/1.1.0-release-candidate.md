## Known deviations and risks

- LocalStack 4.14.0 is an emulator. It does not establish IAM, quota, throttling, latency, or
  regional behavior on AWS.
- LocalStack accepts some malformed EventBridge patterns that AWS may reject. Framework-side
  envelope/schema validation remains the deterministic supported contract.
- Image vulnerability/license disposition must come from the hosted immutable-digest scan. Local
  Maven verification is not a substitute.
- No resolved secret, credential, receipt handle, raw authorization value, or proprietary artifact
  may be retained in candidate evidence.
